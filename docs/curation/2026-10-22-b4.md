**Curator:** Chris Overall. Research done with LLM assistance (Claude). The two policy decisions (iguratimod, herbal products) were made by the curator. The rows apply those decisions, and each was checked against its cited source.

These match the policy for the NSAIDs already listed (etoricoxib, celecoxib, naproxen and others): symptomatic therapy is never counted as the thing a trial tests.

**Iguratimod excluded as a csDMARD (curator decision).**
- ChEMBL gives it three targets: MIF, TRAF3IP2 and COX-2.
- It is an approved conventional DMARD in China and Japan, and in recent trials it is often background therapy alongside tofacitinib.
- It is treated like leflunomide and hydroxychloroquine.
- The alternative, keeping the ChEMBL mapping, was declined. Its COX-2 target overlaps the NSAIDs, and its mechanism is diffuse.
- Cost: its one labelling trial, NCT00965757 (T-614 + MTX vs placebo + MTX), stops being a target contrast.

**Aminopterin mapped to DHFR** (`manual_mechanisms.csv`).
- ChEMBL has the molecule but no mechanism.
- This follows batch b1, which mapped the antifolate CH-1504 to DHFR. Methotrexate itself stays excluded as the anchor therapy.
- The mapping and the exclusion pull in different directions. It is recorded in the row's notes.

**Rabeximod deliberately unmapped.** The company reports that it has identified a target protein but doesn't name it.

**Herbal and supplement products deliberately unmapped (curator decision).**
- Policy: these are therapeutics with no single molecular target, recorded in `unmapped_decisions.csv`. Their trials stay `unmapped` by decision, not by gap.
- This replaces the first precedent, which excluded the Kunxian capsule as "not a therapeutic" (batch 2026-10-13-initial). That override row is removed, and `kunxian` gets an unmapped decision instead.
- It matches batch b3, which treated the plant extract YRA-1909 and hydroxytriptolide the same way.
- Tripterygium is not mapped to the targets of triptolide, its main active compound. The curator declined that option because the link is low-confidence.
- Products (18 names):
  - Tripterygium wilfordii Hook F, TwHF, Tripterygium wilfordii
  - Fang Yi Qing Feng Shi, Huang Qi Gui Zhi Wu Wu, Juanbi pill, Qing Re Huo Xue, Sen Miao San
  - lingzhi, moringa, Colquhounia root, Wangbi granules, ginkgo biloba extract, Kunxian
  - "TCM" and "Integrative Medicine" (one TCM regimen)
  - homeopathic Rhus toxicodendron 30
  - borage seed oil
- Each row cites the trial that uses the product, with the trial title as evidence.
- "Combination fish oil" is mapped to fish oil, which ChEMBL lists as a supplement with no target.

## Effect on the mapping

The mapping, worklist and evidence steps were rerun on 2026-10-22; the AACT pull was not rerun, because this batch doesn't change which trials are included. The new outputs were compared trial by trial with the outputs saved just before this batch (`backups/*_20261022_before_b4.csv`). 19 trials changed class or targets.

**Labelling trials (5):**

| Trial | Before → after | Why |
|---|---|---|

| NCT01724931 (aminopterin) | unmapped → target contrast on DHFR | aminopterin mapped |
| NCT00965757 (T-614 = iguratimod + MTX vs placebo + MTX) | target contrast → excluded-therapy | iguratimod excluded |
| NCT00113308 (GW-406381) | single-intervention → excluded-therapy | NSAID excluded |
| NCT00170872 (lumiracoxib) | single-intervention → excluded-therapy | NSAID excluded |

**Context and ongoing trials (14):**
- Iguratimod trials:
  - 5 became excluded-therapy.
  - NCT04928066 and NCT05626348 went from head-to-head to uncontrolled. Only tofacitinib or the TNF inhibitor remains as a tested drug.
  - NCT06945666 kept its class but lost the iguratimod targets.
  - NCT03855007 went from uncontrolled to unmapped. Its other arm is "HCQ", an abbreviation that doesn't yet resolve.
- NSAID trials: the valdecoxib, aceclofenac and aspirin trials became excluded-therapy.
- The two Kunxian trials (NCT02764515, NCT07721142) went from excluded-therapy to unmapped. They are now unmapped by decision, not excluded.

**Labelling contrast tests: 420 → 420** (404 target contrasts, 16 head-to-heads). The aminopterin trial was gained and the iguratimod trial lost. The low-dose IL-2 trial changed type but still counts.
