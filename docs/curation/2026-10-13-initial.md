# Curation batch 2026-10-13-initial

The curation done while the trial mapping was first built (2026-10-09 to 2026-10-13), recorded after the fact when the provenance columns were introduced on 2026-10-22.

**Curator:** Chris Overall, with LLM assistance (Claude).
**Files:** all rows then in `manual_mechanisms.csv` (13), `intervention_overrides.csv` (46), and `excluded_therapies.csv` (42).

## How these rows came about

- **Excluded therapies.** Policy decisions made while classifying trial contrasts. Conventional synthetic DMARDs, glucocorticoids, NSAIDs, analgesics, gastroprotection, vaccines, and supportive care are background or symptomatic therapy, so a trial is never counted as testing them. Methotrexate is excluded as the anchor therapy, even though ChEMBL gives it a target (DHFR). Each row states its category and reason.
- **Overrides.** Added while working through the names that failed to match ChEMBL:
  - abbreviations (`mtx`)
  - biosimilar codes, mapped to the originator molecule
  - fixed-dose combinations
  - development codes (RC18, SM03, NNC0109-0012, AP1189, HZT-501), with a source URL
  - EXCLUDEs for drug classes, procedures, imaging agents, and drugs treating a complication rather than RA (eprodisate, recombinant alpha-fetoprotein)
- **Manual mechanisms.** Drugs ChEMBL lacks or lists without a mechanism. Each was found by web search and checked against its source, which was recorded in the `source` column.

## What the migration changed (2026-10-22)

- In `source`, parenthetical notes such as "(company claim)" and "(source says JAK, isoform not specified)" moved to `notes`, leaving the URL alone.
- Override URLs that sat inside `note` moved to `source`.
- `source_type` and `confidence` were assigned from the source each row already cited. Overrides without a URL (abbreviations, drug classes, name cleaning) were marked `self-evident`.
- `evidence` is empty for these rows: quotes were not recorded at the time. This batch is listed as legacy in `curation.py` (`LEGACY_BATCHES`), the one batch allowed to omit evidence.
- No mapping changed. Every row maps to the same target or name as before.

## Known limits

- **resomelagon (AP1189):** a biased melanocortin receptor agonist, with receptor subtypes unconfirmed, so `gene_symbols` is empty and confidence is low.
- **tll-018:** the source is the trial record itself. The JAK1/TYK2 target pair was recorded at the time; confidence medium.
- **suciraslimab and natrunix:** supported by a vendor page and a company claim respectively; confidence medium.
