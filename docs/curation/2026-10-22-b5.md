# Curation batch 2026-10-22-b5: trials whose primary endpoint isn't efficacy

**Curator:** Chris Overall. Found with LLM assistance (Claude) during labelling. The policy and each exclusion were decided by the curator.

**Scope:** three trials in the labelling development set (`labels/dev_set_20261022.csv`) whose registered primary endpoints don't measure efficacy.

## Why

The labelling model answered `no_results` for these trials. The reason wasn't missing outcome data: none of their registered primary endpoints measures efficacy, so no efficacy label can be attached to them.

**Policy (curator decision, 2026-10-22):** trials whose registered primary endpoints all measure something other than efficacy are excluded from the efficacy benchmark. Examples:
- pharmacokinetics
- safety or adverse events
- vascular imaging
- mechanistic biomarkers

This follows the policy of batch b2, which excluded trials that treat a complication rather than RA disease activity.

From labelling v3 onwards, the labeller flags such trials with `primary_is_efficacy: false` (`docs/labelling.md`). That flag is how new ones will be found. Each needs a row here before it leaves the benchmark.

## Trial exclusions (`excluded_trials.csv`, 3 rows)

| Trial | Drug | Registered primary endpoint |
|---|---|---|
| NCT03028467 | otilimab (GSK3196165) | Pharmacokinetics (Cmax, AUC, Tmax, half-life) and safety |
| NCT03052322 | MSB11022 (adalimumab biosimilar) | Hypersensitivity adverse events of special interest |
| NCT00679510 | rosuvastatin (RORA) | Carotid intima media thickness |

Each row cites the trial registry, and its evidence is the registered primary outcome as it appears in the trial's evidence packet.

**Still candidates, not yet excluded.** These were found in a second set of 20 trials that was drawn by mistake and is now parked in `labels/extra_20261022/`:
- NCT03926195: filgotinib, semen parameters
- NCT01754935: VX-509, MRI and arthroscopic biopsy
- NCT02164214: etanercept, TWEAK modulation

They need their primary endpoints checked before exclusion.

## Effect on the mapping

The full pipeline was rerun on 2026-10-22 (pull, mapping, worklist, evidence; outputs stamped 20261022). It was compared with the outputs saved just before this batch (`backups/*_20261022_before_b5.csv`).

- **Trial exclusions applied:** 33 → 36.
- **Trial sets:** the three trials moved from `labelling` to `excluded`. Labelling trials went from 828 to 825.
- **Labelling contrast tests: 420 → 417.** All three were target contrasts.
- **Evidence tiers for the 417 tests:**
  - primary analysis: 149
  - primary results without a p-value: 91
  - publication only: 64
  - stop reason only: 15
  - none: 98
- **The flag works on the dev set.** Labelling v3 set `primary_is_efficacy: false` on all three excluded trials. It also flagged two more dev-set trials whose registered primary endpoints are safety only:
  - NCT00995449 (KB003): a repeat-dose safety run-in
  - NCT01612377 (z102): a difference in adverse-event incidence

  Both are candidates for the next batch, together with the three listed above.
