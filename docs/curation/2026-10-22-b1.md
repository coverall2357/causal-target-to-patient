# Curation batch 2026-10-22-b1

**Curator:** Chris Overall. Research done with LLM assistance (Claude). Each row was checked against its cited source, and the batch was reviewed and accepted by the curator before being added.
**Scope:** drugs that ChEMBL identifies but gives no mechanism for, in labelling trials.

## Worklist

- Built from the mapping outputs stamped 20261013, after the name-cleanup commit. At that point 220 labelling trials were unmapped.
- 59 tested molecules had no ChEMBL mechanism, 42 of them in labelling trials. `scripts/curation_worklist.py` now reproduces this list (kind `no_mechanism`).
- The molecules sorted into:
  - targeted investigational drugs (researched in this batch)
  - aliases for drugs ChEMBL already has
  - non-drug or supportive items to exclude
  - cell therapies and non-labelling antibodies (deferred)

## Method

1. For each targeted drug: web searches for the drug name or code with "mechanism" or "target", preferring a paper, trial record, or company source.
2. Where a code was used, the code-to-name link was confirmed through the ChEMBL synonyms in the local ChEMBL cache:
   - SHR0302 = ivarmacitinib
   - ATI-450 = zunsemetinib
   - AMG 592 = efavaleukin alfa
   - NI-0101 = paridiprubart
   - ABBV-154 = adalimumab fosimdesonide
   - LY3871801 = ocadusertib
   - CH-4051 = CH-1504 L-isomer
3. The cited page was then fetched again to take a verbatim quote for `evidence`. Where a page couldn't be fetched (PubMed Central blocked automated access), the paper's title from its DOI or publisher record was used, and `notes` says "Paper title".
4. Trials whose indication looked wrong were checked against the trial title in the AACT pull.

## Corrections made during the batch

- **MK-8457 also inhibits ZAP70.** The first draft listed only SYK. The conference abstract states both, so a ZAP70 row was added.
- **Efavaleukin's first source was wrong.** The first draft cited a Frontiers article that turned out to be about low-dose aldesleukin, not efavaleukin. It was replaced with a source about AMG 592.
- **Fludarabine was nearly excluded as pre-treatment conditioning.** NCT00001677 tests methotrexate + fludarabine as an RA treatment, so it was taken off the exclusion list and deferred.
