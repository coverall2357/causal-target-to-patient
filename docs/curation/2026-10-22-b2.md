# Curation batch 2026-10-22-b2: tightening the RA definition

**Curator:** Chris Overall, with LLM assistance (Claude). The definition change and every trial exclusion were reviewed and accepted by the curator.
**Scope:** which trials count as RA trials. One code change (`RA_STUDIES_CTE` in `sources/aact.py`) and one new curated file (`curated/trials/excluded_trials.csv`).

## Why

Curation batch b1 exposed non-RA trials among the target contrasts, mostly Sjögren's syndrome. `scripts/check_ra_definition.py` (new in this batch) reports, for every eligible trial, how it entered the RA definition. Its run on 2026-10-22 against the 1,340 eligible trials, under the definition in use until then, gave:

| Route | Trials | Labelling contrast tests | What they are |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mesh_direct` | 1,202 | 367 | "Arthritis, Rheumatoid" mapped from the trial's own conditions |
| `mesh_ancestor` | 105 | 27 | Only an ancestor term: Sjögren's syndrome, Felty syndrome, adult-onset Still's disease and others sit under "Arthritis, Rheumatoid" in MeSH |
| `text_adult` | 22 | 8 | Adult RA named in words MeSH didn't map ("Active Rheumatoid Arthritis") |
| `text_juvenile` | 11 | 3 | Juvenile RA only |

The old definition took any MeSH level and any condition text containing "rheumatoid arthritis". One juvenile trial (NCT00029042, "Juvenile Rheumatoid Arthritis") matched MeSH directly, so juvenile trials had to be caught by their condition names, not by the route.

## Definition change

A trial is an RA trial if:
1. "Arthritis, Rheumatoid" is mapped **directly** from one of its listed conditions (`mesh_type = 'mesh-list'`), **or** a listed condition names adult RA in words; **and**
2. it is **not juvenile-only**. A trial is juvenile-only if a condition names juvenile, childhood or pediatric arthritis (or JIA/JRA) and no condition names adult RA.

Trials listing several conditions stay in. `ra_trials_*.csv` now carries `n_conditions` and `conditions`, so labelling can decide whether a trial reports an RA-specific result. The diagnostic found 151 such trials among the kept routes, 15 of them labelling contrast tests.

The old definition is kept as `BROAD_RA_STUDIES_CTE`, so `check_ra_definition.py` can keep showing what the current definition removes.

## Trial exclusions (`excluded_trials.csv`, 13 trials)

These are trials in RA patients that treat a complication rather than RA disease activity, so they don't test a target against RA:
- **Interstitial lung disease (6):** NCT02808871, NCT00578565, NCT04311567, NCT05246293, NCT07643038, NCT06189495
- **Osteoporosis (2):** NCT02944799, NCT00138983
- **Dry eye (2):** NCT00025818, NCT01393132
- **AA amyloidosis (1):** NCT00035334
- **Corneal keratoprosthesis (1):** NCT01582880
- **Anemia of chronic disease (1, fatigue outcome):** NCT00236678

**How they were found:** a keyword scan of trial titles among the kept routes (interstitial, lung disease, amyloid, osteoporosis, anemia, dry eye, cornea, cardiovascular, atherosclerosis, pain, fatigue, sleep, depression, periodontal), followed by review of each match. The scan is **not exhaustive**. Further complication trials may surface at labelling and should be added here in a later batch.

**Kept on purpose:**
- **Trials of RA drugs measuring cardiovascular or atherosclerosis outcomes**, for example tocilizumab vs. etanercept in patients with cardiovascular risk factors.
- **Trials of RA drugs measuring pain**, for example BIORA-PAIN.

They test RA therapeutics in RA patients. Whether their primary endpoint counts as an efficacy label is decided at labelling.

Excluded trials aren't dropped: `pull_ra_trials.py` sets their trial set to `excluded` and records the reason in `exclusion_reason`, and it reports any listed trial missing from a new pull.

## Related changes in this batch

- `files.py`: `latest_dated()` and `stamp_of()`. Every script now finds its inputs as `<prefix>_YYYYMMDD.csv` exactly, so backup copies (`*_before_*.csv`) can no longer be mistaken for data. That mistake broke `curation_worklist.py` earlier on 2026-10-22.
- The batch b1 log's "Effect on the mapping" section, which hadn't reached the repository when b1 was committed, is restored.

## Effect on the trial universe

Fresh AACT pull on 2026-10-22 (outputs stamped 20261022), compared trial by trial with the 20261013 pull.

**Removed by the new definition: 117 trials. Eligible trials went from 1,340 to 1,223.**

| Route under the old definition | context | labelling | ongoing | Total | Were labelling contrast tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mesh_ancestor` (Sjögren's and relatives) | 10 | 66 | 29 | 105 | 27 |
| `text_juvenile` | 1 | 9 | 1 | 11 | 3 |
| juvenile-only with a direct MeSH match (NCT00029042) | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |

The removals match the diagnostic exactly: every ancestor-only and juvenile-only trial left, and no other trial did.

**Moved to trial set `excluded` by `excluded_trials.csv`: 13 trials** (10 labelling, 1 context, 2 ongoing). One was a labelling contrast test: NCT02944799, alendronate for osteoporosis in RA.

**Changes made by AACT itself since 20261013:**
- no trials added or dropped
- one trial changed status: NCT06567470 (CIT-013), from recruiting to completed, so it moves from `ongoing` to `labelling`; it is still unmapped
- no trial present in both pulls changed contrast class

**Net effect on labelling contrast tests: 405 → 374.** That's −30 from the definition and −1 from the trial exclusions; by class, 359 target contrasts and 15 head-to-heads.

**Other counts after the change:**
- Evidence tiers for the 374 tests:
  - primary analysis: 145
  - primary results without a p-value: 85
  - publication only: 60
  - stop reason only: 9
  - none: 75
- Unmapped labelling trials: 159.
- Curation worklist:
  - 10 molecules without a mechanism (3 in labelling trials)
  - 319 names with no ChEMBL molecule (200 in labelling trials)

**Still listed but kept:** 15 trials whose conditions mention Sjögren's, juvenile arthritis or Still's disease also list adult RA. Most are trials covering several diseases. They stay in by design, with `n_conditions` and `conditions` recorded for labelling.
